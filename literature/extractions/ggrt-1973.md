### E-L3-1 Bering 2011 对本文的评价与定位（**L1（对 Bering）/ L3（对 GGRT）**）

来源：Klaus Bering, *A Note on Angular Momentum Commutators in Light-Cone Formulation of Open Bosonic String
Theory*, **arXiv:1104.4446v2**（页边条纹逐字 `arXiv:1104.4446v2 28 Feb 2012`；标题页日期栏印 `April 29, 2022`
= arXiv 重编译日期，**不是论文日期**；11 页；Masaryk University）。本项目**逐页读图**了 pp.1–2、7–11（L1）。

**(a) p.2 §1 Introduction 逐字（关键句）：**

> "One of the most fundamental facts of Bosonic string theory, that a student of string theory would want to
> rederive for himself, is the critical dimension `D = 26`. There are traditionally four\* ways to detect the
> critical dimension `D = 26` in Bosonic string theory.
> 1. Preservation of Lorentz covariance in the light-cone formulation **[3, 4]**.
> 2. No negative norm states/ghosts in the covariant formulation [5].
> 3. The vanishing of the conformal/Weyl anomaly in Polyakov's path integral formulation [6].
> 4. Nilpotency of the BRST generator in the covariant formulation [7]."

脚注 \*（p.2）逐字：「Plus various **heuristic arguments** typically involving **zeta function regularization** [1, 2].」

> "**The original papers of Goddard, Goldstone, Rebbi and Thorn [3, 4], who splits in creation and annihilation
> parts, are sparse on details**, although recently an explicit calculation has appeared in Ref. [8] for the closed
> string case."

> "Students of Zwiebach's book [2], which uses the light-cone formulation, will notice that **the book in Section
> 12.5 stops short of proving† the critical dimension `D = 26`**."

脚注 †（p.2）逐字：「Ref. [1] gives a proof by **sandwiching** `[J^{−i}, J^{−j}]` between a bra and a ket,
**which depend on the choice of vacuum**.」（[1] = GSW Vol. 1）

脚注 ‡（p.2）逐字：「Note that **Goddard, Goldstone, Rebbi and Thorn [4] call the center-of-mass position `x₀^μ`
for `q₀^μ`**.」 ⇒ 佐证 Bering 确实读过本文正文（记号级细节）。

**(b) Bering 的参考文献表（p.11 逐字，本项目直接可用的书目数据）：**

**三条书目层面的净收获（全部 L1，可直接进 `sources.bib`）：**
1. **本文页范围 109–135 得到第二个独立确认**（INSPIRE + Bering [4]）——Polchinski 只印起始页 109。
2. **`goddard-thorn-1972` 的止页是 238**（Polchinski 书末只印 "B40, 235"）。
3. **`goddard-rebbi-thorn-1972`（Nuovo Cimento **12A** (1972) 425–441）本项目此前完全没有登记**，
   而 Bering 把「光锥洛伦兹协变 ⇒ `D=26`」这条路线**同时**归给它和本文（`[3, 4]`）。

**(c) Bering 自己算出的结果（p.7，**加框**的 (7.1)，L1 逐字）：**

> **(7.1)**　`[J^{−i}, J^{−j}] = (2ℏ²/(α₀⁺)²) Σ_{n≠0} : α^i_{−n} α^j_n : κ^{|n|} [ n( (D−2)/24 − 1 ) − (1/n)( (D−2)/24 − a ) ] + O(κ−1) ,`
>
> "which, in the limit `κ → 1`, **precisely vanishes for `D = 26` and `a = 1`**"

- ⚠ **原文措辞是 "precisely vanishes for"，不是 "if and only if"、也不是 "necessary"。**
  （不过括号里的两个系数相互独立，必要性在数学上是显然的——**但那是我们的推断，不是原文陈述，不得写成原文措辞**。）
- 摘要（p.1）逐字：「We recalculate in a systematic and pedagogical way one of the most important results of Bosonic
  open string theory in the light-cone formulation, namely the `[J^{−i}, J^{−j}]` commutators, which together with
  **Lorentz covariance**, famously yield the critical dimension `D = 26` and the normal order constant `a = 1`.
  We use traditional transverse oscillator mode expansions … We streamline the proof by introducing a novel
  **bookkeeping/regularization parameter `κ`** to avoid splitting into creation and annihilation parts, and to avoid
  sandwiching between bras and kets.」
- 结构：§7 证 (7.1)（分 Part 1 / Part 2），§8「Commutator `[E^i, E^j]` via 3-symmetrizer」给 **(8.1)**，
  p.10 末逐字「Finally, if one adds up contributions from eqs. (8.3), (8.4), (8.6) and (8.7), one derives precisely
  the commutator (8.1).」两处证明都以 `□` 收尾。
- ⚠ **`κ` 是 Bering 自创的正规化/记账参数**，`κ → 1` 是取极限；引用 (7.1) 必须带这个前提。
