### 0.4 ★★ 第 13 章为什么没取到（**不是权限问题**）

- 我们**有**这本书的访问权：同一本书、同一个端点的第 10 章（38 页，9.1 MB）**一次成功**。
- 已排除的绕行方案（逐条实测）：
  - `assets.cambridge.org` 同路径 → `Socket is closed`（与既有记录一致）。
  - 去掉尾部文件名 `.../content/view/<HASH>/<file>_CBO.pdf` → 同样超限。

### E1 ★ 第 10 章的自我定位：弦论侧「当且仅当 SO(32)」+ 三处自承缺口

- 位置：p. 143（§10.1 之前的引言段）
- 类型：proposition（元陈述）
- 原文（**逐字，整段**）：
  > "Study of ten-dimensional theories with `N = 1` supergravity seems to reveal that they are all afflicted with hexagon anomalies; for instance, the hexagon diagram with only external gauge bosons receives a possibly anomalous contribution from gluinos, and this contribution is nonzero for every nonabelian gauge group. These points will be further elucidated in chapter 13. Here, after a brief discussion of anomalies in `D = 10` super Yang–Mills theory, we study the analogous issues in string theory. In fact, we study the hexagon diagram with external gauge bosons in type I superstring theory. **A surprising result will emerge: the anomaly cancels if and only if the gauge group is `SO(32)`.** An analysis of the mechanism behind this result in chapter 13 will show that it can be interpreted in field theoretic language in terms of the existence of additional anomalous diagrams beyond that of fig. 10.1b. **This argument suggests that the other hexagon anomalies also cancel for the type I theory with gauge group `SO(32)`, although the relevant calculations have not been performed.** It also indicates the possible existence of an anomaly-free ten-dimensional superstring theory with gauge group `E₈ × E₈`, and indeed this clue played a role in stimulating the discovery of the heterotic superstring theory, which was described in chapter 6."
- 文献自称的严格程度：主结论 **"A surprising result will emerge: … if and only if"**（断言）；但同段**三处自我限定**：`seems to reveal` / `suggests` / **`although the relevant calculations have not been performed`**。
- 依赖：本章 §10.2（自证）；chapter 13（外包）
- 备注（**对图非常重要**）：
  1. **这条 "iff" 只覆盖「纯规范六边形反常，type I，单圈」**，且群的候选集只有 Chan–Paton 允许的 `SO(n)`/`USp(n)`/`U(n)`（见 E5）。**它不是「十维 N=1 反常自由规范群唯一是 SO(32)」**。
  2. `E₈ × E₈` 在本章只是 "possible existence … indicates"，**没有弦论侧计算**。
  3. 与 `green-schwarz-1984` E17 对照：GS 1984 说弦论侧 "details will be presented elsewhere"；**本章就是那个 "elsewhere" 的教科书版**（GS 的原始文章是 Nucl. Phys. B255 (1985) 93–114，本章未在读到的页面内注明出处）。

### E5 弦论侧三类图的 Chan–Paton 群论因子

- 位置：pp. 152–153，(10.2.1)(10.2.2)(10.2.3)
- 类型：definition + proposition
- 原文（逐字转录）：
  - (10.2.1) `G_P = n tr(λ₁λ₂…λ₆)` —— 平面图（annulus，`n` 来自无粒子附着的那条边界上的单位矩阵迹）
  - (10.2.2) `G_N = η tr(λ₁λ₂…λ₆)` —— 不可定向图（Möbius 带，只有一条边界，故**无** `n` 因子）

- `η` 的定义（逐字）："where `η` is a factor due to twisting an odd number of lines (**−1 for `SO(n)`, +1 for `USp(n)` and 0 for `U(n)`**), as explained in §6.1."
- 关于 `U(n)`（逐字）："**Since `U(n)` groups have already been shown to be inconsistent with supersymmetry, they do not need to be considered (although the anomaly is yet another argument for their inconsistency).**"
- 文献自称的严格程度：定义 + 引 §6.1
- 备注：**`U(n)` 在弦论侧被提前排除**（理由是超对称，不是反常）。所以 E6 的 "and for no other group" 实际是在 `SO(n)` 与 `USp(n)` 里挑。
